## Full description

You do the task, the business provides the service.

Passla is not about money transfers — it's skill exchange. Salons, cafés, gyms and more offer their own services when you deliver design, content, or social media work. You submit; they issue a coupon. Show the QR at the venue.

**How it works**
• Discover tasks from businesses near you
• Apply to tasks that match your skills and message the business
• Upload your delivery; after approval the digital coupon lands in your wallet
• Visit the business, show the QR, redeem the service

**For businesses**
Post tasks, find talent, manage applications, verify coupons with QR. Not ad spend — real delivery in exchange for your own service.

**Who is it for?**
Students, freelancers, creators — anyone who wants skill-based connections with local businesses.

**Your privacy**
Location is used only while the app is open to show nearby tasks.
